CASE 01 / Work experience · Support scope

Office network & systems support

Supporting a multi-device workplace across Windows, domain-connected systems and office connectivity.

Tools / technologies

  • Windows Server 2022
  • Windows
  • Domain-connected workstations
  • Satellite internet
  • Mesh Wi-Fi

Situation

An office environment used Windows Server 2022, domain-connected workstations, satellite internet, mesh Wi-Fi and wireless access equipment.

My responsibility

Support users, workstations and office connectivity, and coordinate technical follow-up when an issue required further assistance.

What I investigated

Workstation connectivity, user access and device connection issues across the office network. The support scope covered the server/client environment and the internet and Wi-Fi equipment used by staff.

Actions taken

Provided day-to-day troubleshooting and device support, considered how workstation and connectivity symptoms related to the wider environment, and coordinated escalation and vendor support where required.

Outcome

Provided ongoing user and systems support in a mixed office-network environment. This case describes the support scope rather than a single completed infrastructure upgrade.

Skills demonstrated

Network diagnostics, systems thinking, user support, escalation and technical communication.

What I learned

A useful support record separates the affected device, the service involved and the observed symptom before deciding on the next action.

CASE 02 / Work experience · Investigation

Website & domain investigation

Investigating a business website reported as “Domain not working” across hosting, DNS and redirects.

Tools / technologies

  • Hostinger
  • Cloudflare
  • DNS
  • Redirects
  • .htaccess

Situation

A business website was reported as unavailable, with a domain-related error.

My responsibility

Investigate the hosting and domain configuration and identify possible causes for technical follow-up.

What I investigated

Hosting status, DNS and Cloudflare configuration, and the possibility of unexpected redirect behaviour involving .htaccess.

Actions taken

Reviewed the reported hosting error and available domain configuration, and considered the relationship between DNS, the hosting service and redirect rules.

Outcome

The investigation narrowed the areas requiring technical follow-up. A completed restoration is not confirmed in the available record.

Skills demonstrated

Structured troubleshooting, DNS fundamentals, hosting support and documentation.

What I learned

Record the observed error separately from a suspected cause. A redirect hypothesis should be verified before it is presented as a diagnosis.

CASE 03 / Work experience · Diagnosis

CCTV recording & storage diagnostics

Checking DVR configuration and storage availability when recordings could not be retrieved.

Tools / technologies

  • DVR interface
  • Storage checks
  • Physical inspection
  • Vendor coordination

Situation

An office surveillance system did not have the expected recordings available.

My responsibility

Check the recording system and storage status and coordinate technical follow-up.

What I investigated

DVR recording settings and storage availability, followed by a physical inspection of the recording hardware.

Actions taken

Reviewed the DVR configuration, checked whether recording storage was available and coordinated the next technical steps with a service provider.

Outcome

The checks identified a storage-availability problem and enabled appropriate technical follow-up. Restoration of recording is not documented here.

Skills demonstrated

Hardware diagnostics, evidence-based troubleshooting, vendor coordination and concise reporting.

What I learned

Storage availability is a distinct check from camera connectivity. A visible camera feed does not by itself confirm that footage is being recorded.
