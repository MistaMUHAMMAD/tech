# CCTV recording & storage diagnostics

Anonymised work experience. Diagnosis.

## Tools / technologies

DVR interface, Storage checks, Physical inspection, Vendor coordination

## Situation

An office surveillance system did not have the expected recordings available.

## My responsibility

Check the recording system and storage status and coordinate technical follow-up.

## What I investigated

DVR recording settings and storage availability, followed by a physical inspection of the recording hardware.

## Actions taken

Reviewed the DVR configuration, checked whether recording storage was available and coordinated the next technical steps with a service provider.

## Outcome

The checks identified a storage-availability problem and enabled appropriate technical follow-up. Restoration of recording is not documented here.

## Skills demonstrated

Hardware diagnostics, evidence-based troubleshooting, vendor coordination and concise reporting.

## What I learned

Storage availability is a distinct check from camera connectivity. A visible camera feed does not by itself confirm that footage is being recorded.
