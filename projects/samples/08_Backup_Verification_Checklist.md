## Verification

- [ ] Confirm that the dataset is included in the approved backup scope.
- [ ] Review the latest job status and record the time and result.
- [ ] Check that required files or services are included, not just that a job completed.
- [ ] Check retention coverage and whether exceptions were reported.
- [ ] Confirm that authorised personnel can access the backup record.
- [ ] Review the last approved recovery-test result.
- [ ] If authorised, restore a test copy to an isolated approved location.
- [ ] Check that the restored item opens and matches the intended test version.
- [ ] Record failures, missing coverage and responsible follow-up owners.
