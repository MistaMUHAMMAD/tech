# Device troubleshooting decision tree

Portfolio Sample | Muhammad Musa | October 2026

Demonstration template. No employer records or real user data.

## Initial checks

1. Record the user-visible symptom, device reference and effect on work.
2. Confirm whether there is a power, damage or electrical safety concern. Stop use and escalate if present.
3. Identify whether the device powers on.

## If the device does not power on

Check the approved power source, external cable and adapter connection. Record indicators and symptoms. Escalate unresolved power/hardware faults. Do not open electrical equipment beyond your authorised service scope.

## If the device powers on

Identify the affected area: operating system, application, peripheral or connectivity. Compare the symptom with a known working state and record the exact error.

## Test and follow through

Use an appropriate low-impact check, obtain approval for changes where needed, record the result and test the original user task. If unresolved, escalate with the checks already completed. Close only after verification or an agreed handover.
