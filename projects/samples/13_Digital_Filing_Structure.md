# Digital filing structure

Portfolio Sample | Muhammad Musa | October 2026

Demonstration template. No employer records or real user data.

## Demonstration folder plan

| Folder | Contents | Owner | Review rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01_Admin | Approved office templates and correspondence | Operations owner | Review template changes |
| 02_IT_Support | Procedures, service summaries and user guides | IT support owner | Review after changes |
| 03_Assets | Device register and maintenance records | Asset-record owner | Update at handover |
| 04_Projects | One folder per approved project | Project owner | Review at milestones |
| 05_Vendors | Quotations and service records | Procurement owner | Review before purchase |
| 06_Reports | Periodic approved reports | Report owner | Review before release |
| 99_Archive | Closed records retained under policy | Records owner | Review retention date |

## Naming pattern

Use a sortable date, subject and version when a date is useful. Example: `2026-10-24_Device_Maintenance_v01.md`. Use `DRAFT` or `APPROVED` only when that status has a defined owner.

## Record index

| Record ID | File name / relative path | Owner | Status | Review date | Access group |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DEMO-DOC-001 | 02_IT_Support/Windows_Support_Guide.md | Demo IT owner | Draft | 2026-11-24 | Demo staff |

## Working rules

- Give each working record one agreed location and owner.
- Separate approved templates from completed operational records.
- Limit sensitive records to the approved access group.
- Record version and review ownership; avoid duplicate final copies.
- Apply the organisation's retention and backup policy.

This is a sample filing plan, not a record of an employer's folder structure.
