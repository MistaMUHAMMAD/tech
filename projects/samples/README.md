# IT support portfolio samples

Fourteen demonstration projects by Muhammad Musa. All sample data, users, devices, vendors, prices and risk ratings are fictional. None of these files is an employer record.

- CSV: open in Excel or Google Sheets. CSV preserves data, not formatting or workbook formulas.
- Markdown: edit in a text editor, or view the formatted version on GitHub.
- SVG: open in a browser or design tool.
- Excel: the workbook includes editable asset, helpdesk, risk and vendor sheets with filters and validation. The prepared table area has 50 data rows. Extend the table, validations and formulas together when adding more.
- The IT risk register is a learning sample, not evidence of formal IT risk employment.

The helpdesk timestamps are fictional local office time (WAT, UTC+1). Calendar-hour closure time is not an SLA or working-hours measure. Risk scores use illustrative likelihood x impact on 1–5 scales; the organisation would define its own criteria. Vendor totals compare the same quantity and include the separately entered delivery and tax amounts. They do not rank quality or recommend a real supplier.

Replace demonstration rows before real operational use. Keep completed records and sensitive configuration out of public repositories.
