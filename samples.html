How to use these samples

Open CSV files in Excel or Google Sheets. Markdown files are editable text and render on GitHub. SVG diagrams open in a browser. The Excel workbook includes asset, ticket, risk and vendor sheets with fictional example rows, filters and editable fields.

The basic IT risk register is a learning exercise. It does not imply formal IT risk-management employment experience.
